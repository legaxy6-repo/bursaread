Glossary

The terms on this site, in plain words

AUD/MYR
How many ringgit one Australian dollar buys.
Basis point (bp)
One hundredth of a percentage point. A move from 3.78% to 3.73% is 5 basis points.
BNM 5pm middle rate
Bank Negara Malaysia's published midpoint between banks' buying and selling rates at 5pm. An official reference, not a rate you can trade at.
BoE rate
The Bank of England's Bank Rate, its main policy rate.
BoJ rate
The Bank of Japan's policy interest rate.
Brent
The global benchmark price of crude oil, in US dollars per barrel.
Bund 10Y
The yield on Germany's 10-year government bond, Europe's reference long-term rate.
CNY/MYR
How many ringgit one Chinese yuan buys.
CPO 3rd month
Crude palm oil futures on Bursa Malaysia Derivatives for delivery three months ahead: the benchmark palm oil price, in ringgit per tonne.
CSI 300
An index of 300 large companies listed in Shanghai and Shenzhen.
DAX
Germany's main stock index: 40 large companies.
Desk inference
The desk's own reasoning about a cause or effect. Never presented as reporting.
Dollar index (DXY)
The US dollar's value against six major currencies, mostly the euro. Up means a stronger dollar, which usually weighs on Asian currencies, the ringgit included.
Dow
The Dow Jones Industrial Average: 30 large US companies, weighted by share price.
ECB rate
The European Central Bank's deposit facility rate, its main policy rate.
EUR/MYR
How many ringgit one euro buys.
EUR/USD
How many US dollars one euro buys.
FBM KLCI
Bursa Malaysia's main index: the 30 largest listed companies by market value.
Fed funds rate
The US Federal Reserve's policy interest rate, set as a range. The figure shown is the top of the range.
FTSE 100
The UK's main stock index: 100 large companies listed in London.
GBP/MYR
How many ringgit one British pound buys.
GBP/USD
How many US dollars one British pound buys.
Gilt 10Y
The yield on the UK's 10-year government bond.
Gold
The price of gold in US dollars per troy ounce.
Hang Seng
Hong Kong's main stock index.
Interbank mid
The midpoint price banks trade currencies at between themselves. Bank board rates for customers include a margin, so they differ.
Intraday
A level taken while the market was still open, not at its close.
JGB 10Y
The yield on Japan's 10-year government bond.
JPY/MYR (per 100)
How many ringgit 100 Japanese yen buy. The yen is quoted per 100 because one yen is worth so little.
KLIBOR
Kuala Lumpur Interbank Offered Rate: the older benchmark built from banks' quoted lending rates. It is being phased out in favour of MYOR.
LPR
China's one-year loan prime rate, the benchmark for bank lending in China.
MGS
Malaysian Government Securities: ringgit bonds issued by the government. The 3-, 5- and 10-year figures are the yields investors earn for lending to the government for that long.
MGS–UST spread
The MGS 10-year yield minus the US 10-year yield, in basis points. Negative means US bonds pay more than Malaysian ones, which tends to pull money toward the dollar.
Moved
A number whose change passed the desk's filter: currencies 0.5%, stock indices 1%, 10-year yields 5 to 6bp, gold 1.5%, palm oil 2%, oil 3%.
MYOR
Malaysia Overnight Rate: a reference rate based on overnight interbank borrowing in ringgit, published by Bank Negara Malaysia. It is replacing KLIBOR as the benchmark for loans and contracts.
Nasdaq
The Nasdaq Composite, a US stock index heavy in technology companies.
Nikkei 225
Japan's best-known stock index: 225 large companies listed in Tokyo.
OPR
Overnight Policy Rate: Bank Negara Malaysia's benchmark interest rate, set at its Monetary Policy Committee meetings. It anchors what banks pay on deposits and charge on loans.
Reported
A line taken from a named news report or official source, linked to the exact page that states it.
S&P 500
An index of 500 large US companies, the usual measure of the US stock market.
SGD/MYR
How many ringgit one Singapore dollar buys. Up means the ringgit weakened against it.
Shanghai Composite
An index of all stocks listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange.
SORA
Singapore Overnight Rate Average, published by the Monetary Authority of Singapore: Singapore's equivalent of MYOR.
STI
Straits Times Index: Singapore's 30 largest listed companies.
Stoxx 600
An index of 600 large and mid-sized European companies.
TOPIX
A broader Tokyo stock index covering most of the market's large and mid-sized companies.
USD/CNY
How many yuan one US dollar buys in mainland China.
USD/JPY
How many yen one US dollar buys. Up means a weaker yen.
USD/MYR
How many ringgit one US dollar buys. Up means the ringgit weakened against the dollar.
USD/SGD
How many Singapore dollars one US dollar buys.
UST
US Treasuries: US government bonds. The 2-year yield tracks where markets expect the Fed's rate to go.
WTI
West Texas Intermediate, the US benchmark crude oil price, in US dollars per barrel.
Yield
The return a bond pays, as a yearly percentage of its price. Bond prices and yields move in opposite directions.

How this works

  • Every line tagged Reported links to the exact page that states it. Lines tagged Desk inference are the desk's own reasoning. A cause no reporter gave reads "no catalyst reported".
  • Ringgit rates are Bank Negara Malaysia's official 5pm middle rates. A number that can't be confirmed is left blank.
  • Analysis and education only. Nothing here is a buy or sell call. Times are Malaysia time (MYT, UTC+8).
