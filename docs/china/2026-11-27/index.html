What it means for Malaysia

Trade channel: China is Malaysia's largest trading partner, and higher US tariffs on China raise the risk that Chinese exports get diverted into ASEAN, adding pressure on Malaysian steel and manufacturers. For now Beijing is choosing talks over retaliation, which keeps the ringgit and Bursa exporters out of the worst case.
