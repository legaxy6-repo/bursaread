What it means for Malaysia

Three channels point the same way for now: a hiking Fed (rates) caps the ringgit, higher Brent (commodities) helps Petronas-linked revenue and oil-and-gas stocks but lifts fuel-subsidy costs, and the tariff proposal (trade) leaves Malaysian exporters waiting until Q1 2027 for a final rate.
