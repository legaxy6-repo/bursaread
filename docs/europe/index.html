What it means for Malaysia

Trade channel: the EU faces a proposed 15% US tariff, which weighs on European demand for Malaysian electronics and palm-oil-linked goods if it sticks. In the near term, Europe's tech rebound helps the global chip mood that Bursa's semiconductor names follow.
