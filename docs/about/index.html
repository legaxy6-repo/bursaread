Sources, rules and corrections

Daily Desk summarises the world's markets each business day for readers new to finance, and explains what the moves mean for Malaysia: the ringgit, Malaysian rates and Bursa.

Sources

  • Malaysia: Bernama, The Star, The Edge, NST, FMT, Business Today, Malay Mail, Bank Negara Malaysia, DOSM, Bursa Malaysia, the Securities Commission.
  • Abroad: AP, Reuters, CNBC, FT, WSJ, Bloomberg, Nikkei Asia, SCMP, The Business Times, and central banks and statistics offices.
  • Never: newsletters without a named reporter, AI-generated market sites, forecast sites, forums.

Reported or inference

Every line tagged Reported links to the exact page that states it. Lines tagged Desk inference are the desk's own reasoning and are never presented as reporting. If no reporter gave a reason for a move, the read says "no catalyst reported".

Numbers

Ringgit rates are Bank Negara Malaysia's 5pm middle rates. Other figures carry their time and source. A number that can't be confirmed is left blank and listed under "Left blank". Market prices are shown as reported, not as a live feed.

Independent check and corrections

A separate process, which did not write the reads, re-opens every linked source each morning and flags any line or number that doesn't match, as a bank's maker-checker control would. Flags appear on the read itself. Reads are never quietly edited: a confirmed error is corrected in the next read, under "Corrected from an earlier read", and the history behind the charts is fixed at the same time.

Not advice

Analysis and education only. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
