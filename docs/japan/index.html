What it means for Malaysia

Currency channel: a yen near 159 per dollar makes Japanese goods cheaper relative to Malaysian exports; a BoJ hike in December would ease that. Japan is also a major investor in Malaysian manufacturing, so a tariff deal that holds near 15% limits the knock-on effect.
