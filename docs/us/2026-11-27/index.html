The US is set to open sharply higher in a thin post-Thanksgiving half-day as battered AI and chip stocks rebound, even as Treasury yields rise with a December Fed hike fully priced.

  • AI rebound: at 08:47 ET Nasdaq futures were up about 2%, S&P 500 futures up 1.4% and Dow futures up 1.2%, with Nvidia up over 3% premarket as AI-linked stocks recovered.
