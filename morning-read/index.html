How this page works

  • The Malaysia read is written each Malaysian business day at about 7:15am MYT, once the US session has closed and Bursa's prior-day ringgit, CPO and BNM figures are out.
  • The US, China, Japan, Singapore and Europe reads are written each weekday at about 6:45am MYT and cover each market's last completed session. The Global read follows straight after and summarises them. A note on the US open is added to the US read at about 10:45pm MYT.
  • Summaries say what happened and the reasons reporters gave. The desk's own reading is kept to lines marked Desk inference, so you can tell the two apart.
  • Every line tagged Reported links to the exact page that states it. Lines tagged Desk inference are the desk's own reasoning, never reporting. A cause no reporter gave reads "no catalyst reported".
  • Sources: Bernama, The Star, The Edge, NST, FMT, Business Today, Malay Mail, BNM, AP, Reuters, CNBC, FT, WSJ. Prices taken from Alpha Vantage are labelled with their time. A number that can't be confirmed is left blank.
  • A number is marked moved when it passes the desk's filter: currencies 0.5%, stock indices 1%, MGS and MYOR 5bp, other 10-year yields 5–6bp, gold 1.5%, CPO 2%, oil 3%.
  • Live check uses your own Alpha Vantage connector, only when you press Refresh: one call per price, from a free key that allows 25 calls a day shared with the scheduled reads. Opening the page costs nothing. Prices are interbank mid rates, not a bank's board rates.
  • Analysis and education only. Nothing here is a buy or sell call. Times are Malaysia time (MYT, UTC+8).
