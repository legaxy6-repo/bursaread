03 / Principles

Calm by design. Strict about facts.

03.1

Every line has a source.

Reported lines link to the article or data page that states them: Bernama, BNM, The Star, AP, Reuters.

03.2

Explains. Never recommends.

No buy or sell calls and no price targets. Daily Desk is for understanding the market.

03.3

Every number carries its time.

Each figure shows when and where it came from. If a number can't be confirmed, it stays blank.
